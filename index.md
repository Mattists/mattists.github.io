## Selected Research
{: #research}

### Spatial Similarity Search

We extend PathSim to compare entities based on both geographic proximity and semantic relationships in heterogeneous networks.  
[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3849732.3857327) · [Code](https://github.com/Mattists/Co-Location-Similarity-Search-with-Semantic-Constraints)

### CaTSCAN

We develop algorithms for exactly counting small, typed subgraphs in heterogeneous networks.  
[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-37657-2_31) · [Code](https://github.com/c-beth/CaTSCAN)

### General Semantic Knowledge Infusion for Spatio-Temporal Traffic Forecasting

We use Wikidata to capture context around traffic sensors, such as nearby points of interest, and incorporate it into graph neural networks for traffic forecasting.  
[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/MDM71479.2026.00016) · [arXiv](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.17440)  · [Code](https://github.com/cau-kiel-ai/Semantic-Knowledge-Infusion-ST-Prediction)
